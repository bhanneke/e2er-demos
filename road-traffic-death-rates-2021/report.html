Abstract

Road traffic deaths remain a major source of preventable mortality, yet the reported burden differs sharply between countries. We ask how the World Health Organization's estimated road traffic death rate varies across countries and WHO regions in 2021. We describe the rate for 195 countries and areas in the WHO Global Health Observatory, summarise it by the six WHO regions, and identify the highest and lowest rates. The median country records 12.3 deaths per 100,000 population, unweighted by population size. The African region averages about three times the rate of the European region, the lowest of the six. Regions separate 39 percent of the variation in national rates. Because the rates are model-based estimates, the pattern describes WHO's modelled figures, which show a wide gap between countries that follows regional lines.
