\begin{abstract}
The Pleiades is a standard nearby reference for the main sequence and the distance scale. This paper asks which stars within three degrees of its centre are members by Gaia DR3 parallax and proper motion, and what their colour-magnitude diagram and parallax distances show. Using $9{,}970$ quality-filtered Gaia DR3 sources, we require a RUWE below $1.4$, a parallax over error above $10$, a parallax within three standard deviations of the cluster median, and a proper motion within $5$ mas/yr of the cluster centre.
