\begin{abstract}
The Pleiades is one of the nearest open clusters, and Gaia gives parallaxes and proper motions for its stars. We ask which stars in a small cone around the cluster centre are members by Gaia DR3 parallax and proper motion, and what the members' distances and colour--magnitude diagram show. A parallax window and a proper-motion radius, both centred by iterative clipping, select 1,112 members from the sources with precise astrometry.

Inverting the uncorrected parallax, the members have a median distance of 135.7 parsecs.
% src: estimation_results.json#distance_summary.distance_pc_median
Their colour--magnitude diagram follows one continuous main sequence from bright to faint stars. The membership count shifts little under single changes to the selection and by up to about a fifth under combined changes. The members form a coherent group at that parallax distance, before any correction for the parallax zero-point.

Keywords: Pleiades, open cluster, Gaia DR3, parallax distance, colour--magnitude diagram.
\end{abstract}
