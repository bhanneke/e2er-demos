\begin{abstract}
Monthly air temperature is dominated by a near-deterministic annual cycle, so any forecasting model must beat ``same month last year.'' We ask how monthly mean 2 m air temperature in Frankfurt am Main has changed since 1981 and whether seasonal models beat a seasonal naive forecast over 36 months. Using NASA POWER data for 1981--2025, we fit trend regressions with autocorrelation-robust errors and compare ETS, seasonal ARIMA and a trend-plus-harmonics regression on a hold-out of 2023--2025.

Annual mean temperature rises by 0.50\,$^\circ$C per decade over 1981--2022, at 0.62 in winter and 0.61 in summer against 0.38 in spring and 0.39 in autumn.
